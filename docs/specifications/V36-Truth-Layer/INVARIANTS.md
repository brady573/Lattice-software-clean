# V36 Invariants

1. **Truth authority:** persisted structured evidence state is authoritative; generated prose is not.
2. **Claim typing first:** proof obligations are bound before factual release.
3. **No source voting:** multiple reports sharing an origin count as one provenance component.
4. **Original-source preference:** recover upstream primary artifacts when practical.
5. **Temporal applicability:** publication date and evidence-effective date are distinct.
6. **Typed proof obligations:** quantitative, causal, current-state, authenticity, and context checks
   cannot be skipped by generic confidence.
7. **Positive burden asymmetry:** unsupported positive claims become `UNVERIFIED`, not `FALSE`.
8. **High-risk corroboration:** causal/authenticity claims require a materially independent chain.
9. **Adversarial search:** important positive claims receive a disconfirming search route.
10. **Contradiction verification:** a contradiction must itself be applicable and traceable.
11. **Bounded research:** follow-up is finite and stops on sufficient evidence or bounded exhaustion.
12. **Parallelism is not evidence:** scheduling concurrency cannot change proof meaning.
13. **Rejected evidence is inert:** rejected/invalid evidence cannot satisfy constraints or scoring.
14. **Same-Run provenance:** final evidence-backed factual statements resolve to admitted evidence
    from the same Run unless an explicitly versioned reusable evidence contract is later qualified.
15. **Uncertainty is a result:** unresolved evidence gaps are explicit, typed, and explainable.
16. **Decision/explanation fidelity:** explanation cannot alter persisted material verdicts or ranking.
17. **Simulation coefficients are seeds:** V36 calibration values are implementation starting points,
    not externally validated epistemic constants.
