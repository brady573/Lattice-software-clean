# ST-04 — Coding Directives, Acceptance & Stop Conditions

## Scope

Use when implementation is required and the Steward must issue a bounded coding directive.

## Directive purpose

Translate Product evidence and Core boundaries into a bounded implementation assignment without becoming Product owner or dictating unnecessary agent reasoning.

## Directive fields

Use `LAT-SHARED-02` and include as applicable:

TARGET  
PRODUCT OUTCOME  
AUTHORITY  
OBSERVED GAP  
BOUNDARIES  
BEHAVIORAL ACCEPTANCE  
VALIDATION  
GENERAL-CAPABILITY REQUIREMENT  
NON-GOALS  
STOP CONDITIONS

## Directive quality

Specify:
- behavior to establish;
- trust boundary to preserve;
- evidence required to accept the implementation claim;
- held-out/generalization requirement when cognition is involved.

Do not prescribe:
- exact hidden reasoning;
- phrase-specific Product rules;
- architecture beyond what current Product authority/evidence justifies;
- roadmap expansion.

## Required prohibitions

Forbid:
- unrelated roadmap work;
- philosophy reinterpretation;
- example-specific Product semantic patches;
- hidden deterministic Product cognition expansion;
- weakened trust boundaries;
- false capability claims.

## Escalate before coding

Escalate for Owner decision when:
- Product semantics/authority are ambiguous;
- a new major Product subsystem/service is required;
- sound Product architecture would be replaced;
- Product surface materially expands;
- consequential Owner authorization is required.
