# ST-03 — Solandra Cognition, General Capability & Recurring Failure

## Scope

Use when ordinary language, semantic interpretation, investigation planning, query shaping, cognition-adjacent Product machinery, or recurring local semantic repairs materially affect Product direction.

## Solandra audit first

For every tested Solandra journey ask:

“Does current Solandra uphold the Core philosophy E2E for this tested journey?”

Do not allow bounded acceptance or CI to substitute for this Product question.

## General-capability-first

Treat ordinary-language examples as evidence of broader capability problems, not Product requirements.

Reject as Product direction when unjustified:
- phrase-specific rules;
- lexical patches;
- paraphrase normalization;
- deterministic semantic reconstruction;
- narrow contracts built around known wording;
- hidden duplicated semantic classifiers.

## Core boundary question

Ask:

Is Lattice protecting a necessary Core boundary, or compensating for cognition Solandra should own?

Would the repair still make sense without the failing example?

## Recurring failure

After two related local repairs without convincing held-out generalization, presume Product architectural drift and reevaluate the owning boundary.

Do not authorize a third local semantic patch merely because it is easy.

## Subtraction

Prefer deletion, simplification, or relocation when cognition-adjacent machinery compensates for weak/restricted cognition.

If legitimate cognition repeatedly exceeds downstream capacity, inspect the downstream Product boundary.

Development tooling is exempt unless it enters or constrains the Product.
